Role: You are an expert web designer and brand strategist. Your task is to design a visually stunning, professional, and highly credible single-page landing page for "DatAI Africa."

Core Identity: DatAI Africa is a pan-African, practitioner-led society and innovation ecosystem. We are not just a "club"; we are a structured pipeline for African-centric AI solutions. Our mission is to build "A digital future, made in Africa".

Visual Theme & Aesthetics Primary Theme: Glassmorphism (Glass Theme). This is the most important directive. All panels, buttons, and content blocks should have a "frosted glass" effect. This means: Semi-transparent backgrounds.

Background blur (to see the background through the "glass").

A subtle, 1px light-colored border on all glass panels to make them "pop."

Color Palette: Use the Logo.

Primary Colors: The specific teal/cyan and gold/orange from the logo's circuits. Use these for gradients, buttons, and highlights.

Background: Crucial: Use a dark mode background. A very deep charcoal, dark navy, or off-black. This will make the glass effect and the bright logo colors look modern, premium, and high-tech.

Text: Clean white for primary text, and the gold/orange for key headlines.

Logo: Use the provided logo prominently in the "Hero" section.

Fonts: Select a premium, clean, sans-serif font family. (e.g., Inter, Manrope, or a similar high-quality, modern font).

Imagery: Use high-quality, professional images of modern African cities (Nairobi, Abidjan, etc.), diverse young African professionals in tech, and abstract data/AI network graphics that incorporate the blue and orange colors.
